CinderX is a CPython extension focused on runtime performance improvements, including a JIT and
Static Python pathways.

For external users, the key is to stay rigorous about runtime identity and benchmark methodology.

<!-- truncate -->

In this repo, we keep comparison policy CinderX-first and require publish guard checks before
sharing headline numbers.
